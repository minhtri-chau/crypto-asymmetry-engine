# v8.9.5.1 hotfix

## Root cause
v8.9.5 added `structuralState()` and `btcDownsideSensitivity()` to `api/research-context.js`, but each function was missing its final closing `}`. Vercel therefore could not parse the serverless function and reported `FUNCTION_INVOCATION_FAILED`, with the module-loader warning pointing at the later `export default` line.

v8.9.5.1 restores both missing braces. `node --check api/research-context.js` passes.

## Also included
- Preserves the deployed Scanner correction: `RETEST / HOLD` requires at least one completed weekly close above resistance. A fresh cross with zero completed weekly closes remains `BREAKOUT ATTEMPT`.
- Adds an explicit Research Context error panel and Retry button.
- Cache-busts the hotfixed Research Context request.
- No scoring changes.
- No SQL migration, Edge Function, Cron, environment variable, or dependency changes.
